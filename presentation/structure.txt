Forward vs Inverse Problems

Microlensing as Inverse Problem
- gravitational lensing, magnification, images, lightcurve
- binary lens, exoplanet, caustics
- use grid search, mcmc to infer posterior
- problems: highly multimodal posterior
- high amount of data due to Roman ST Survey

Struggles of Bayesian Inference: MCMC
- convergence computationally expensive
- stuck in degeneracies e.g. wide-close degeneracy (grid searches expensive, human interference)
- likelihood evaluation (expensive --> needs simulator output, unavailable --> approximated with gaussian)
- show likelihood in Bayes equation p(theta|x) = p(x|theta) * p(theta) / p(x)

SBI 
- learns to relate x to posterior p(theta|x)
- does not need to evaluate likelihood (likelihood-free), likelihood is implicitly inside simulator
- just needs to sample x from a simulation (dependend on theta)
- uses a simulation to generate these samples
- so: replaces Bayes equation with NN (Neural Posterior Estimation)
- variations NLE, NRE
- apply the network on observed data to estimate the posterior of the model parameters

Benefits (Comparison)
- likelihood-free (no likelihood evaluation)
- amortized inference
- learns multimodal posterior distributions directly (with degeneracies, network aware of entire posterior)

Simple Example 
- Ball getting thrown at angle theta

Training SBI
- choose prior p(theta)
- sample theta from prior
- generate dataset (x, theta)
- horizontals are like posteriors in this dataset (but difficult in high dimensions)
- train network on paired dataset
- loss function, E(-log(q(theta,x))), essentially cross entropy with p(theta,x)
- network architecture, NPE, Normalizing Flows

Evaluation of observed data in example (show code)
- compare/show posteriors
- corner plots or so

Limitations (Comparison)
- gives only a neural approximation of the posterior
- no quality of posterior (for MCMC you can test convergence)
- calibration of posterior difficult (over-/underconfident)
- cases not in simulation give nonsensical results (for MCMC convergence again)
- simulation has to approximate the real world physics, dependence on correct simulator

- 10^5 posterior samples per second
