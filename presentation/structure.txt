Struggles of classical Methods: Fit/MCMC
- convergence computationally expensive
- stuck in degeneracies e.g. wide-close degeneracy (grid searches expensive)
- Likelihood evaluation (expensive, unavailable --> approximated with gaussian)
- show likelihood in Bayes equation p(theta|x) = p(x|theta) * p(theta) / p(x)

SBI 
- replaces Bayes equation with NN
- learns to relate x to posterior p(theta|x)
- does not need to evaluate likelihood (likelihood-free)
- just needs to sample x from a simulation (dependend on theta)
